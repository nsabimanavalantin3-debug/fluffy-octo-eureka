About SomaRwanda

SomaRwanda is a study platform for secondary students in Rwanda. The goal is to help students learn, revise, and prepare for exams.

It covers core subjects across Science, Arts, and other common combinations from S1 to S6.

  • ✅ Lessons organized from S1 to S6
  • ✅ Clear subject and topic lists
  • ✅ Easy navigation between levels
  • ✅ Simple design for daily study
